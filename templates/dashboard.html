How KaamSetu works

Job applications by email, sent from your own Gmail, with a resume that matches each job.

  1. Add recruitersPaste emails or import a CSV, Excel, Word, PDF or screenshot. You review everything before it's saved.
  2. Write your emailStart from a role template or let AI draft one from the job post. Names and companies fill in for each person.
  3. Tailor your resumeGet an ATS match score for a job description and accept or decline each suggested change. It stays one page.
  4. Send safelyPreview every email, confirm once, and KaamSetu sends in small batches within your daily limit.
